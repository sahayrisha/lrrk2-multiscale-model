# AI Usage Disclosure

Claude (Anthropic) was used as a development tool throughout this project. All scientific conclusions, model design decisions, and results are the author's own. AI assistance was limited to research support and debugging, with every output reviewed and independently verified before use.

Specific uses included:

- Literature search and source identification, including locating candidate held-out validation datasets (Bae et al. 2018, Fan et al. 2018, Xilouri et al. 2013/2016, Bieri et al. 2019) and verifying their independence from existing calibration sources
- Drafting initial versions of validation, diagnostic, and figure-generation scripts, subsequently reviewed, adapted, and run by the author
- Debugging assistance, including identifying an optimizer seeding error in the uncertainty quantification pipeline
- Assisting in reasoning through the structural cause of the validation discrepancy

All code in this repository was reviewed, adapted, and executed by the author. All reported numerical results were generated by running the author's own code. An initial hypothesis proposed during diagnosis of the V2/V3 finding was later found incomplete upon further testing; the mechanism ultimately reported was independently verified against model output before inclusion.
